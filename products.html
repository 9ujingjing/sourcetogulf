Three Lines, One Sourcing Partner

Custom apparel, fashion jewelry and custom packaging — designed, sampled and made in China, shipped to the Gulf with landed prices you can plan around.

Three lines we actually run

Not a marketplace of 30 million things. Three lines where we can put your brand on it, sample it, and reorder it fast.

Line 01

Custom apparel — small batch, fast reorder

Abayas, kaftans and modest wear made to your design. Sampling in days, and reorders that do not sit behind someone else's container.

  • Fabric library on the wall — pick the hand feel and colour, not a catalogue photo
  • In-house sampling room: pattern, cut, stitch, press
  • Your own woven label, care label, size tag and hangtag
Line 02

Fashion jewelry — design to sample

316L stainless steel, PVD gold plating and moissanite. Send a sketch or a photo and we turn it into a physical sample you can hold before you commit.

  • 316L stainless steel base — tarnish-resistant, no green finger
  • PVD gold plating and GRA-certified moissanite
  • Your logo on the pouch, the box and the jewellery tag
Line 03

Custom packaging — designed, printed and made in-house

Folding cartons, mailer boxes, rigid gift boxes, drawer boxes and paper bags. Printing, die-cutting, gluing and pulp inserts all happen in the same factory.

  • Printing, die-cutting, gluing and pulp inserts on site
  • Structure design from a flat dieline — not just printing your logo
  • Hot foil, emboss, spot UV, lamination — we will tell you which are worth it
