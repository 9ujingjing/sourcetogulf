Shipping to Kuwait

Method: Sea freight, DDP to door (双清到门 — double customs-cleared). For unbranded, non-electric goods — these clothing styles qualify.
Rate: RMB 2,500 / CBM (≈ KD 115.00 / CBM). Final delivery fee depends on the drop-off address.
Transit: ~50–55 days after vessel departure. China–Kuwait cargo volumes are low, so consolidation takes time — once goods reach our warehouse, about 2 weeks until the container loads.
Rate note: 1 RMB ≈ KD 0.046 (indicative, for reference only).

Terms & Contact

Price basis: FOB China, per piece/set. Prices shown in Kuwaiti Dinar (KD); RMB reference kept. Sample orders from 1 pc per style.
Lead time: in-stock styles ship in 3–5 days; bulk orders ~10–15 days.
Branding: we put your brand on the packaging and labels — ask for options.
DDP quote: message us the style numbers + quantity on WhatsApp and we reply with your all-in door price.
