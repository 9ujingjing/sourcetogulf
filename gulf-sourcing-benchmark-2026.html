How this benchmark was built

Transparent method, so you can trust the numbers and reuse them.

Data source. SourceToGulf's live 2026 product catalogue — 150 SKUs, 7 categories, last updated 2026-09. This is the same inventory we quote against, not a modelled estimate.

Rate model. FOB converted at CNY 7.15 per USD. Air freight follows the Cainiao model: CNY 68 for the first 0.5 kg plus CNY 45.6 per extra kg, divided by 7.15. Duty is 5% of the CIF value (goods + freight) under the GCC Common Customs Law. VAT is applied per country — UAE 5%, Saudi Arabia 15%, Oman 5%, Bahrain 10%, Kuwait and Qatar 0% — on goods + freight + duty.

What "typical landed" is not. It is a planning baseline at median order size. A real quote also includes supplier sourcing, quality inspection, custom/private-label packaging, consolidation and compliance handling (SABER for Saudi, ECAS for the UAE), delivered as one all-in price with no separate commission. What this means for a Gulf small buyer

Three things the data settles.

Low-MOQ is real

Start under 100 units

Median MOQ sits at 48–100 across most categories, and plus-size modest fashion starts at 5. Small first batches are the norm, not a favour a factory grants you.

Speed is built in

95% ship within 10 days

Production windows are short across the catalogue. That is what makes fast reorders — and testing a new SKU with low risk — practical for a small shop.

Cost is predictable

Land under $5, or ~$30

Accessories typically land at USD 2–5 per unit all-in; modest apparel at USD 27–30. The calculator turns your exact spec into a number.
