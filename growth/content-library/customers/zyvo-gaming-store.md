# Oman gaming accessories shop — testing the China market at 1-piece MOQ

**Who (anonymized):** A Muscat gaming shop owner who messaged on WhatsApp wanting mouse, mousepad, headset, RGB, keyboard accessories — low price, small batch to test the market.

**The real situation:** Two China suppliers covered mice/keyboards/headsets, but **neither had mousepads** — the exact item he asked for. We sourced it separately (Shenzhen/Yiwu, MOQ 50–100, ¥5–20).

**The pricing lesson (content gold):**
- Entry products: Gulf retail is 3–5× CNY, so 2× markup was *too low* → recommend 3×.
- Gaming products: 2–3× is healthy.
- Air freight to Muscat: AED 35 base + AED 23.4/kg. For a 0.1kg mouse, shipping > 4× the product cost → "order 5+ items to share shipping, per-item drops to AED 5–10."

**Why it's content gold:** This is the perfect "we source *anything you send a photo of*" proof. The mousepad gap became a trust moment ("not in stock, sourcing now, quote in 48h") — exactly the positioning that beats other AI-recommended suppliers.

**Outcome:** Quote delivered (EN + AR). Awaiting logo-on-packaging upsell and mousepad source confirmation.
