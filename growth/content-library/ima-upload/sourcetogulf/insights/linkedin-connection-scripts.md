# LinkedIn 加人备注怎么写（可直接复制）

## 三条原则（决定了接受率）
1. **前 15 个字符最关键**——手机上对方只看到开头，第一句必须有"我为什么找你"的具体信息，不能是套话。
2. **提一个具体细节**（对方的城市 / 公司 / 刚发的帖子 / 做的品类），接受率能翻 2–3 倍。
3. **不推销**。第一句话绝不能是"我想帮你从中国采购"——那是被拒最快的写法。定位成"同行交流 / 备用资源"。

## 模板 A · 海湾小卖家 / 电商卖家（Salla / Zid / IG 小店老板）
```
Hi [Name], I work with small brands in the Gulf on sourcing from China — mostly helping first-time importers figure out MOQs and landed cost before they commit. Saw what you're building at [X]. Happy to share what I'm seeing on the factory side if it's ever useful. — Robin
```

## 模板 B · 采购 / Supply Chain 从业者
```
Hi [Name], I'm on the China side of Gulf–China sourcing — factories, QC, shipment consolidation. Always useful to know people who actually run procurement in [Dubai/Riyadh]. Not selling anything, just comparing notes on lead times and supplier vetting. — Robin
```

## 模板 C · 创业者 / 想做自有品牌的人
```
Hi [Name], I help small Gulf brands put their own label on products made in China — sampling, packaging, low MOQ. If private label is on your roadmap at any point, happy to be a resource. Following what you're doing at [X]. — Robin
```

## 模板 D · 通用版（批量加人时用，无需个性化）
```
Hi [Name], I'm Robin — based between Guangzhou and Dubai, working on China→Gulf sourcing for small brands. Always good to connect with people building things in the region. Happy to compare notes anytime. — Robin
```

## 发送节奏与红线
- 每天 **15–20 个**即可，不要一次群发几十个。
- 🔴 **被点了太多 "I don't know this person" 会被 LinkedIn 限流甚至限制邀请功能**。宁可少加、加准。
- 🔴 对方接受后 **不要立刻甩广告 / 报价 / 链接**。等 1–2 天，或先正常回一句。上来就推销 = 被解除连接或被举报。
- ✅ 对方接受后的第一句可以是：`Thanks for connecting — if you ever need a sanity check on a China quote, just message me. No pitch.`
- 优先加：海湾本地小卖家、电商运营、采购/供应链、零售创业者。
- ❌ 不加：同行 sourcing agent / 货代（加了没用，还可能抄打法）。

## 为什么这步比发帖重要
账号连接数太少时，LinkedIn 算法没有分发对象，发帖展示量会极低（实测首帖仅 15 展示）。先把连接数拉到 150+，发帖才有意义。

# 评论怎么写（比发帖更重要的曝光动作）

## 评论四要素（缺一个就是废评）
1. **补一个对方没说的数据/事实** — 证明你真懂这行
2. **给一个从业者视角** — 从供应链/工厂端看到什么（这是我们的独特角度）
3. **提一个真问题** — 引发对方或其他人回复，回复 = 曝光再翻一倍
4. **署名带定位** — `— Robin, China→Gulf sourcing`，让看评论的人知道你是谁、能点你主页

❌ 不要写："Great post!" / "Thanks for sharing" / 纯 emoji / 硬推销 / 放 WhatsApp 号码（LinkedIn 评论放号码很掉价，可能被删）。

## 时机（决定一半效果）
- 🔴 **帖子发出后 2 小时内评论**，曝光红利最大。截图里那种 "1 周前" 的帖子红利已过——但说明该号在持续发帖，**关注它，下次新帖秒评**。
- 优先选：**点赞 < 20 的新帖**（评论能被顶上热评），避开已有 100+ 评论的爆帖。

## 实战模板 · 沙特零售媒体号（27k 关注，行业新闻型）

**A. 针对「沙特时尚电商 Al-Hasoub 利润 +50.5%」这类增长新闻**
```
Interesting — a 50% profit jump in fashion retail usually traces back to the supply chain, not just demand. The Gulf brands growing fastest right now are the ones cutting their China→KSA lead time and reordering in smaller, more frequent batches. Inventory efficiency is the quiet driver nobody posts about. — Robin (China→Gulf sourcing)
```

**B. 针对「TABI 融资 2.33 亿美元」这类 BNPL/金融新闻**
```
BNPL growth like this usually shows up on the supply side a few months later — more small Gulf sellers confident enough to place a bigger first order. Their bottleneck stops being capital and becomes sourcing: finding factories that accept low MOQs and actually hold a delivery date. Are you seeing that shift yet? — Robin (China→Gulf sourcing)
```

**C. 针对「Zid 是战略合作伙伴」这类平台生态新闻**
```
Zid has done a lot to lower the barrier for Saudi sellers to launch. In my experience the next bottleneck is almost always the product side — sourcing, private label, getting a real sample in hand before committing. That's the gap we work in from the China end. Good to see the ecosystem filling in. — Robin
```

## 频率与分寸
- 每天 **3 条**，其中**最多 1–2 条**能自然带出自己的业务角度，其余纯专业观点（不然像刷广告）。
- 评论对象优先级：海湾零售/电商行业媒体号 > 本地品牌创始人 > 电商平台官方号。**不加同行 sourcing agent。**
- 评论里可以提行业数据、工厂端观察，但**永不提客户名、永不报价、永不发 WhatsApp**。
