# Dubai Bulk Stationery Order

**What it is (anonymous):** A bulk stationery quotation for a Dubai reseller — a long tail of small items (pens, notebooks from AED 0.80) plus at least one large consolidated line (AED 23,740).

**The real lesson:** A first bulk order is rarely one SKU. It's a spread:
- Cheap repeatables (pens/notebooks, AED 0.8–11)
- Mid items (AED 50–300)
- One or two whale lines (AED 3,200 / 23,740)

**Why it's content gold:** Most "how to import" content shows a single product. This shows the *real* shape of a Gulf reseller's first order — and how consolidating small + large lines into one DDP shipment is what actually saves them money. Fully anonymizable.

**Outcome:** Quoted. Strong "behind a real bulk quote" asset for LinkedIn/IG carousels.
