# Dubai boutique perfume brand — from loose items to a gift-box system

**Who (anonymized):** A small UAE perfume brand selling bottled perfume, oud chips, and an incense burner — all as loose retail items, no packaging concept. Reached out via WhatsApp wanting to "organize it with a box" and think "out of the box."

**The real pain:** He wasn't buying a box. He wanted a *concept* that makes him look like a premium Gulf fragrance house on Instagram. His references showed two directions: a retail display acrylic case, and a velvet octagon gift box with gold foil.

**What we proposed (a system, not one box):**
- **A · Signature Discovery Box** — double-drawer, foil logo, EVA insert. MOQ 500, FOB ¥8–15, retails AED 89–149.
- **B · Heritage Velvet Octagon** — wood base + velvet, gold foil. MOQ 200, FOB ¥35–70, retails AED 250–450 (Ramadan / Eid / wedding).
- **C · Emirati Modest Set** — magnetic box, 3 pcs (perfume + lotion + soap). MOQ 500, FOB ¥12–22, retails AED 120–180.

**Why it's content gold:** A complete delivery story (concept → mockup → sample → launch) is the strongest "real supply chain" proof we have — far more convincing than anything we self-produce. The customer's own unboxing video (once launched) is the single best UAE-differentiation asset.

**Outcome:** In sampling flow. First response scored on "we actually read your references" + "composite sourcing partner, not a freight forwarder" + no early quote + Eid urgency.
