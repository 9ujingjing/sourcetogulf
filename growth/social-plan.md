# Social Media & Backlink Plan — SourceToGulf (P5)

> Audience: the four buyer personas — **influencers/KOLs, small business
> owners, side-hustle moms, resellers** — across UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Kuwait,
> Bahrain, Oman.
>
> Every post should *earn a backlink* to the site (bio link + link in
> caption/stories to a relevant guide or persona page).

---

## Platforms & roles

| Platform | Role | posting cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Top-of-funnel for influencers & resellers; factory tours, product picks, "ship samples" proof | 3–4 / week |
| **Instagram** | Brand trust; Reels (unboxing, packaging reveal), Stories (Q&A), bio link | 3 / week |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B credibility; landed-cost explainers, SABER checklists, founder POV | 2 / week |
| **WhatsApp Broadcast** | Convert warm leads; new arrivals, restock alerts | as needed |
| **Facebook Groups** | Community answer-box; link guides, not pitches | 2–3 / week |

## Content pillars (rotate)

1. **Factory & sourcing proof** — "we found this in Guangzhou today", supplier
   verification, QC clips. Builds the *composite-capability* story.
2. **Samples → bulk** — unboxing of sample shipments, "approve before you buy".
3. **Landed-cost education** — mini explainers linking to
   `/blog/landed-cost-china-to-gulf-explained.html` and `/#calculator`.
4. **Persona spotlights** — "mom started a home-fragrance side hustle",
   "influencer launched a private-label line" → link the matching persona page.
5. **Gulf-market tips** — SABER for KSA, Ramadan/Eid seasonal buying, free-zone
   vs mainland. Link the relevant import guide.

## Backlink mechanics

- **Bio / link-in-bio** always points to `https://sourcetogulf.com`
  (use a UTM: `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social`).
- **Caption links** go to the *most relevant* page (guide / persona / calculator),
  not the homepage — deeper links earn deeper indexing.
- **Stories** → "link sticker" to a persona page during campaigns.
- Repurpose every blog post into 1 TikTok + 1 LinkedIn + 1 community answer.
- Track clicks via GA4 UTM; double down on the persona that converts.

## Tie-back to SEO / GEO

Social shares don't directly rank you, but they:
- drive referral traffic (a ranking signal),
- earn natural mentions/links from creators (see backlinks-plan.md),
- feed AI engines fresh, real-world proof of your composite capability.
